Prof. Yucheol Cho

Yucheol Cho received his Ph.D. from KAIST in 2025.

He earned his master’s degree in 2020 from KAIST, specializing in semiconductor device simulations. Afterward, he worked at Samsung Electronics until 2021, where he developed a strong interest in deep learning.

His doctoral research focused on data engineering, multimodal models, knowledge distillation, and anomaly detection. After his Ph.D., he worked at the Agency for Defense Development (ADD).

He is currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Hanbat National University.
