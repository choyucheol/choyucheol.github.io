Research Topics

Computer Vision

We explore advanced computer vision techniques for object recognition, detection, and visual understanding. Our research focuses on representation learning and efficient architectures to tackle complex visual perception tasks in real-world scenarios.

Multimodal Model

Our work investigates models that integrate diverse modalities, including vision, language, and audio. By aligning and fusing these modalities, we aim to achieve richer understanding and more robust AI systems for practical applications.

Time Series Anomaly Detection

We develop algorithms for detecting anomalies in time series data, enabling early detection of irregularities in critical systems such as finance, healthcare, and manufacturing. Our methods emphasize both accuracy and scalability.

AI for Physics

We apply machine learning to accelerate physics research, from materials simulation to fundamental scientific discovery. Our research bridges domain knowledge and AI techniques to open new frontiers in computational physics.
